About Us

Welcome aboard, mate!

Pier Capitan Resto Bar & Grill is where Ormoc comes to unwind. Tucked at Stall #102 of the Superdome Building in Brgy. South, we're a laid-back restobar built around three simple things: honest food off the grill, drinks poured right, and a crew that makes every table feel like regulars.

Whether it's after-work beers with the barkada, a family dinner, or a full-blown celebration, the Captain has a seat saved for you. And when you can't come to us, our Mobile Bar brings the party to your doorstep.

  • From the grillSmoky, charcoal-kissed Filipino favorites.
  • Signature drinksCocktails, towers, shots & ice-cold beer.
  • Events & Mobile BarBirthdays, weddings, corporate nights.
